KRides ("Krides", "we", "us", "our") operates the KRides mobile app, connecting student riders with independent drivers for rides around campus and the surrounding area. This explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have — written for riders and drivers alike, since one app serves both. By using KRides, you agree to this policy.

1

Information we collect

What you give us

  • Account details — name, phone number, email, and password (or your Google account, if you sign in with Google).
  • Driver details — vehicle info, verification documents, and bank account details for payouts.
  • Ride content — pickup/destination, ratings, and anything written in a report or support message.

What we collect automatically

  • Location — see Location data.
  • Ride history — fares, timestamps, payment method, driver/rider pairing.
  • A push-notification token tied to your device, plus basic technical data (app version, device type).

What we don't store

We never see your full card number — card payments are tokenized and processed directly by Flutterwave.

2

Location data

KRides needs your location to find nearby drivers, calculate a route, and let riders and drivers track each other during a ride.

  • Riders — used for your pickup point and to show your driver's live position mid-ride.
  • Drivers — shared with a matched rider for that ride, and used while "online" to match you with nearby requests.

You can turn off location access in your device settings, though the app can't match or track rides without it. We never sell location data.

3

How we use it

  • Creating and securing your account, and verifying driver eligibility.
  • Matching riders with nearby drivers and calculating fares.
  • Processing payments, wallet top-ups, driver payouts, and refunds.
  • Sending ride updates, receipts, and account notifications.
  • Responding to support requests and investigating reports.
  • Keeping the platform safe — detecting fraud and abuse.
4

5

Payments & your wallet

Card payments are processed by Flutterwave, a licensed payment processor. We store your transaction history and wallet balance; your card details stay with Flutterwave's secure systems.

Drivers' bank details are shared with Flutterwave solely to send ride earnings by transfer — we keep only what's needed to initiate and reconcile payouts.

6

Push notifications

Your device's push token is used for ride status, matches, and account alerts. It's cleared on sign-out and re-registered on sign-in, so notifications always follow the account currently signed in — never a previous one on the same device.

Turn these off any time in your device settings.

7

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Deleting your account removes your profile, ride history, and documents — except records we must retain for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons.

8

Your rights & choices

  • Access & correct — update your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account — in-app, under account settings. Removes your profile and driver details, including bank information, subject to the retention note above.
  • Location & notifications — controlled in device settings.
  • Questions — email us, see Contact.
9

Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our database enforces access rules so one user's account can't read or modify another's. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10

Children's privacy

KRides is for users aged 15 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger — contact us if you believe a younger person has an account and we'll remove it.

11

International transfers

We operate primarily in Nigeria, but some providers we rely on — like Firebase/Google Cloud — process data outside Nigeria, under their own security and compliance standards.

12

Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date above whenever this policy changes. Continuing to use KRides after a change means you accept the update.
