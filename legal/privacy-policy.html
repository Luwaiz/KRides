KRides ("Krides", "we", "us", "our") operates the KRides mobile app, a platform that connects student riders with independent drivers for rides around campus and the surrounding area. This policy explains what we collect through the app, why we collect it, and the choices you have — written for both riders and drivers, since one app serves both.

By creating an account or using KRides, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If anything is unclear, email us — the address is at the bottom of this page.

01Information we collect

What you give us directly

  • Account details — full name, phone number, email address, and password (or Google account details, if you sign in with Google).
  • Driver details — vehicle information, identification/verification documents, and bank account details used to pay you for completed rides.
  • Ride content — pickup and destination addresses, ratings you give or receive, and anything you write in a driver report or support message.

What we collect automatically

  • Location — see Location data below.
  • Ride history — fares, timestamps, payment method, and which driver and rider were matched.
  • Device data — a push-notification token tied to your device, and basic technical data (app version, device type) used for debugging.

What we don't store

We never see or store your full card number. Card payments are tokenized and processed directly by our payment processor, Flutterwave — see Payments.

02Location data

KRides needs your location to function: to find nearby drivers, calculate a route, and let riders and drivers track each other during an active ride.

  • Riders — your location is used to set your pickup point and to show your driver's live position while a ride is in progress.
  • Drivers — your location is shared with a matched rider for the duration of that ride, and used while you're marked "online" to match you with nearby ride requests.

You can disable location access in your device settings at any time, but the app won't be able to match rides or track trips without it. We don't collect or sell your location data for advertising.

03How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and verify driver eligibility.
  • To match riders with nearby drivers and calculate fares.
  • To process payments, wallet top-ups, driver payouts, and refunds.
  • To send ride updates, receipts, and account notifications.
  • To respond to support requests and investigate reports filed against a driver or rider.
  • To keep the platform safe — detecting fraud, abuse, and policy violations.

05Payments & your wallet

All card payments are processed by Flutterwave, a licensed payment processor. KRides stores your transaction history and wallet balance, but your card details are handled entirely by Flutterwave's secure systems.

Drivers' bank account details are shared with Flutterwave solely to send ride earnings via bank transfer. We retain the minimum driver banking information needed to initiate and reconcile those payouts.

06Push notifications

We use your device's push-notification token to send ride status updates, driver/rider matches, and account alerts. This token is cleared from our systems when you sign out, and a fresh one is registered when you sign in again — so notifications always follow the account you're currently signed into, never a previous one on the same device.

You can turn off push notifications at any time in your device settings.

07How long we keep it

We keep your account and ride data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your profile, ride history, and stored documents from our systems, except where we're required to retain certain records — such as payment and transaction records — for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

08Your rights & choices

  • Access & correct — update your profile details directly in the app.
  • Delete your account — available in-app under account settings. This permanently removes your profile and driver details, including stored bank information, subject to the legal retention note above.
  • Location & notifications — controlled through your device's permission settings.
  • Questions or requests — email us at the address in Contact us and we'll respond.

09Security

Data in transit between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our database applies access rules so that, for example, one user's account cannot read or modify another's. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect your information.

10Children's privacy

KRides is intended for users aged 15 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone younger. If you believe a younger person has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

11International transfers

KRides operates primarily in Nigeria, but some of the services we rely on — such as Firebase/Google Cloud and our hosting provider — process data on servers located outside Nigeria. Where that happens, those providers maintain their own security and compliance standards for handling your data.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll update the "Effective" date at the top of this page when we do. Continuing to use KRides after a change means you accept the updated policy.
